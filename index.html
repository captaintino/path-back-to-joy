A poem & Bible study

The Path Back to Joy

Written during a postpartum low — and the search for joy that followed.

I wrote this poem during a postpartum low that lasted 10 months. I knew the Bible taught about joy during suffering, but I couldn't understand how to experience it.

So I prayed for wisdom and truth about my suffering and joy. And I searched the Bible for the answers. I read those passages over and over. Some I even memorized. As I learned, I wrote this poem to speak these lessons back to myself. I always find that internalizing God's trustworthy truth, love, and hope proves to be transformative.

And God has also taught me I will not always be joyful in this life. Not even the teacher of contentment, the Apostle Paul, was always joyful. The Bible describes how God provides for and guides His children during those tough times. Now I can face my weak times more prepared, more assured, more at peace, more aware of the rich Hope I have inherited from Jesus' salvation. I can lean on what I learned from the Bible, and I can repeat this poem to myself to help me remember the steps back to joy.

I pray this poem encourages you. Even more, I pray you read the Bible passages referenced so you can be strengthened by God's good words.

— J. Ashley Carroll, Psalm 40

Cold rain
Soaked the pain
Of my soul.

Laughter glimmered
In and out,
But no hold.

We were learning
And growing,
God's blessings overflowing.

But with rain in my eyes
And unshakeable chill inside,
I cried,

"Where is joy?
Where is joy?
Why won't it stay?"

Then I searched Your good word,
And the Spirit guides me to pray

(Galatians 5:22-25, Ephesians 6:17-18)
